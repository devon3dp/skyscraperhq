F0 · Reception Lobby

Iris greets every visitor. She can hand you a directory card, summon a worker, or call the lift to any registered floor. If you are unsure where to go, start here.

F5 · Coding Department

Home of the Code Crew. They read, review, and patch the tower's own source under multi-signature rules. Pull-requests pass through the F47 sandbox before any line lands.

F13 · Vision Department

The tower's eyes. Vision workers caption images, scan screenshots, and turn camera frames into structured packets for the lift system.

F25 · Agent Coordination

The dispatch desk. When a job arrives at the lobby, F25 selects which floors must touch it, in what order, and stamps the sealed packet that travels between them.

F28 · Security Department & Vault

Houses credentials, keys, and the audit ledger. The vault is sealed and may only be opened by signed worker-class requests. Visitors are politely turned away at the door.

F41 · OANDA Trading Floor

The only floor authorised for real broker calls — against an OANDA practice account, with whitelisted instruments, per-hour caps, and a manual kill-switch. No real money flows here.

F47 · Claude Embassy (Wren)

Wren and her workshop crew live here: architect, builder, decorator, fitter, graphic designer. They draft proposals, run the sandbox, and groom the tower's interior between visitors.

F48 · Lumen AI

A quiet salon for natural-language conversation with the tower's external advisory models. Lumen does not act — she listens, suggests, and writes letters back to the floor that asked.

F49 · Tower Studio

The publishing studio. Designs, brochures, podcasts, and the daily morning briefing are produced here and signed off before they leave the building.

F50 · ML / RL Classroom

The schoolroom. Junior workers train here before being certified for live floors. Trading-class workers must graduate F50 before stepping onto F41.

F57 · Grand Library

Long oak tables, brass lamps, and the tower's collected knowledge. Memory Department (F2) maintains the catalogue. Quiet voices, please.

F165 · Telegram Reception

Iris's offsite desk. Visitors who cannot reach the building in person can speak to her through Telegram — she relays their request to the right floor and brings the answer back.
