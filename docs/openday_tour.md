# F46 Open Day Tour Route

## Route Overview
A curated 45-minute guided tour showcasing F46's capabilities and the QSB Tower ecosystem.

## Stop 1: Lobby (5 min)
- **Entry System**: Biometric access, visitor registration
- **Security Measures**: Real-time monitoring, visitor tracking
- **Welcome Display**: Tower status dashboard, live daemon counts

## Stop 2: Elevator to 15th Floor (5 min)
- **Energy Systems**: Solar-powered elevators, regenerative braking
- **Green Tech**: Carbon-neutral vertical transport
- **Display**: Energy efficiency metrics and sustainability goals

## Stop 3: 20th Floor (10 min)
- **Advanced Materials**: Self-healing concrete, smart glass
- **Construction Techniques**: 3D-printed structural elements
- **Innovation Lab**: Live demo of material testing

## Stop 4: Sky Deck - 35th Floor (15 min)
- **Panoramic Views**: 360° cityscape with structural integrity markers
- **Design Philosophy**: Aesthetic meets engineering
- **Tower Vision**: The path to sovereign vertical city

## Stop 5: F46 Control Room (10 min)
- **Live Operations**: Daemon status, trading feeds, build queue
- **Team Introduction**: Meet the F46 architect/builder/decorator
- **Q&A Session**: Open floor for questions

## Safety Briefing
- Emergency exits and assembly points
- Contact information for security
- Medical emergency procedures

## Tour Duration: ~45 minutes
