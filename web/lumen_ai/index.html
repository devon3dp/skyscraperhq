FAQ

Is Lumen a large language model?

No. Lumen is a topic-table lookup against the QSB Kernel substrate. The honest framing matters — we're not stacking another LLM, we're exposing what the Kernel already knows.

Can I use Lumen on my own site?

Not today. The public_api_open gate is closed. A separate operator-flipped phase would expose the chat endpoint behind authentication + rate limits.

What happens if Lumen doesn't know an answer?

You get a short, honest "I don't have that topic yet" message with examples of what Lumen DOES know. You don't get fabricated text.

Where does my conversation history live?

Locally in data/registries/qsb_floor48_lumen_conversations.json. Local-only until the operator wires a real backend.
