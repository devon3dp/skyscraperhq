# Wren's Trading Annex Design

## Overview
Wren's Trading Annex is designed to provide a personalized trading environment with advanced features tailored for efficient market analysis and execution.

## Layout
- **Central Dashboard:** A large screen displaying real-time market data, charts, and notifications.
- **Workstations:** Multiple workstations equipped with high-performance laptops and ergonomic chairs.
- **Storage Area:** Secure storage for important documents and physical assets.
- **Break Room:** Comfortable seating area with refreshments to maintain focus during long trading sessions.

## Features
- Customizable dashboard widgets for market data, news feeds, and alerts.
- High-resolution monitors for detailed chart analysis.
- Advanced trading algorithms integrated into the system.
- Secure access controls and encryption for data protection.
