# Sketch — Kettle Corner Relocation

*Pinned to the Architect's Drawing Table, draft status.*

A small but stubborn problem. The Kettle Break Area currently sits beside the Atelier wall, and the rolling boil carries across into colour conversations. The sketch shifts the kettle, sink and biscuit shelf to the south alcove near Reception, where ambient hum is already welcome. Plumbing reroutes are minor. The decorator approves; the architect wants a noise study before signing.
