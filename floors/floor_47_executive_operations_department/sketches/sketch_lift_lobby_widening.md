# Sketch — Lift-Lobby Widening

*Pinned to the Architect's Drawing Table, draft status.*

A plan view. The current lobby admits one guest at a time; on Open Day the queue forms back into the lift car itself. The proposal pushes the partition ninety centimetres east, taking from the Social corner and giving to the Reception threshold. Two guests would then disembark abreast without brushing shoulders. Floor finishes carry across cleanly. Awaiting sign-off.
