# Blueprint File — Floor 47 Plan

*A concierge note from Floor 47, Claude Embassy.*

The Blueprint File is a flat oak drawer set into the wall just past the Reception Desk. Pull it open and you will find a single large sheet — the current plan of the floor — kept up to date as rooms are added. Twenty-five rooms today; more may join in time. Guests are welcome to glance at it on their way through; it is also reproduced below for convenience.

The plan is grouped by **zone**, not by build order. A visitor walking the floor from the lift can follow the zones in a natural arc: arrive through Reception, drift west into the Design Studios and Atelier, north into the Workshops, then around through Audit, Operations and Archive, finishing at the Social corner and the Vista window.
