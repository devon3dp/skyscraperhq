# Sketch — North-Window Panel Reorganisation

*Pinned to the Architect's Drawing Table, draft status.*

A study in pencil on tracing vellum. The proposal lifts the radiator panel from the south wall and re-hangs it on the east return, freeing the full north elevation for a single tall window. Morning light would reach the Atelier swatch lamps without crossing the workshop bench. The architect has marked one margin query — sill height — and otherwise judges the proportions sound.
