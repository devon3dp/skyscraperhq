# Wren's Trading Annex Design
## Overview
Wren's Trading Annex is designed to provide an efficient and user-friendly environment for trading operations.

## Layout
1. **Dashboard Area**: Centralized real-time market data, portfolio performance, news updates, and customizable alerts.
2. **Trading Desk**: High-performance workstations with dual monitors for executing trades, analyzing charts, and managing orders.
3. **Meeting Room**: A dedicated space for discussing trading strategies and reviewing performance metrics.
4. **Storage Space**: Secure storage for important documents and equipment.
5. **Support Services Area**: IT support and finance services to ensure smooth operations.
