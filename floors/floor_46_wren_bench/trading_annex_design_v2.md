# Wren's Trading Annex Design (v2)
## Overview
Wren's Trading Annex is designed to provide an efficient and user-friendly environment for trading operations.

## Layout
1. **Dashboard Overview**: A central dashboard with dynamic floor cards for real-time data visualization.
2. **Trading Desks**: Enhanced trading desks with interactive 3D models and real-time market updates.
3. **Support Services**: Integrated support services panel offering quick access to help resources.
