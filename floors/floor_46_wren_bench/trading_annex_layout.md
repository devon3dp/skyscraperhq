# Wren's Trading Annex Layout

## Key Areas:
- **Trading Desks**: 
  - Located in the central area for easy access and collaboration.

- **Meeting Rooms**:
  - Positioned near the entrance for quick meetings and discussions.

- **Storage Spaces**:
  - Placed along the perimeter to minimize disruption during trading activities.

- **Support Services**:
  - IT and finance support areas are strategically located for immediate assistance.
