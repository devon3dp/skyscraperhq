# Wren's Trading Annex Design
## Overview
Wren's Trading Annex is designed to provide a robust and efficient trading environment tailored for the needs of F46 operations.

## Layout
- **Trading Floor**: Central area with multiple workstations equipped with high-performance computers and market data feeds.
- **Monitoring Room**: Adjacent room for real-time monitoring of trades, risk management, and compliance checks.
- **Breakout Area**: Comfortable seating and refreshment station for traders to take breaks.

## Features
- High-speed internet connectivity
- Advanced trading software and tools
- Secure data storage and backup solutions
- Real-time market data feeds
