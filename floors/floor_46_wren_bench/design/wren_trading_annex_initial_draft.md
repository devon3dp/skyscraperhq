# Wren's Trading Annex Design
## Overview
The Trading Annex is designed to provide a dedicated space for trading activities, equipped with advanced tools and resources.

## Key Components
- **Trading Dashboard**: Central hub for monitoring trades and market data.
- **Simulation Lab**: Environment for testing trading strategies.
- **Risk Management Tools**: Systems for assessing and mitigating risk.
- **Market Intelligence Hub**: Access to real-time market insights.

## Layout
The layout will feature large collaborative spaces with whiteboards and video conferencing capabilities, along with private desks equipped with high-resolution monitors and ergonomic seating.
