# Wren's Trading Annex Design

## Overview
Wren's trading annex is a custom-built space designed to optimize workflow and enhance productivity for trading activities. The design focuses on ergonomics, aesthetics, and functionality.

## Layout
- **Entrance Area:** Welcoming area with information desk and seating.
- **Trading Floor:** Open-plan workspace with individual desks and collaborative areas.
- **Meeting Rooms:** Private rooms for client meetings and strategy sessions.
- **Break Room:** Relaxation space with kitchenette and lounge area.

## Features
- Customizable workstations with adjustable monitors and ergonomic chairs.
- High-speed internet connectivity and robust security measures.
- Advanced trading software and data visualization tools.
- Comfortable seating and relaxation areas to promote well-being.
