## Rug
No rug — polished concrete floors for tripping safety in high-traffic workspace.

## Seating
Lightweight aluminum stools — flexible, durable, easy to move for reconfiguration.

## Materials
- Polished concrete floors (gray finish)
- White walls for brightness
- Adjustable-height desks
- Under-desk storage units

## Layout
- Open plan maximizing natural light
- Central collaboration zone with metal shelving
- Potted plants in gray ceramic pots for warmth
- LED task lighting mounted above workstations

## Palette
Gray, Charcoal, Cream — minimalist, professional, inviting.

## Rationale
F46 is Wren's floor. Decisions made: no rug (safety), stools (flexibility), concrete (durability), open plan (light). Her F46 team synthesized.
