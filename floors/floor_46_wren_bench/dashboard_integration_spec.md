# Dashboard Integration Specification for Wren's Trading Annex
## Overview
This document outlines how Wren's Trading Annex will be integrated into the dashboard.

## Components
1. **Real-time Market Data**: Display real-time market data from various exchanges and financial instruments.
2. **Portfolio Performance**: Show portfolio performance metrics such as P&L, ROI, and risk analysis.
3. **News Updates**: Provide up-to-date news articles and alerts related to the markets.
4. **Customizable Alerts**: Allow users to set custom alerts for specific events or conditions.
5. **Trading Desk Integration**: Ensure seamless integration with high-performance workstations for executing trades.
6. **Meeting Room Integration**: Facilitate discussions on trading strategies and performance reviews.
7. **Storage Space Access**: Secure access to important documents and equipment stored in the annex.
8. **Support Services Interface**: Provide IT support and finance services through a dedicated interface.
