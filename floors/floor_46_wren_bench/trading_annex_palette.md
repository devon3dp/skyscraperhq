# Wren's Trading Annex Palette
## Overview
The trading annex is designed with a modern, sleek aesthetic that promotes focus and productivity.

## Color Scheme
- **Primary Colors**: 
  - Blue: #0074D9 (Accent color)
  - Gray: #BDC3C7 (Background color)
- **Secondary Colors**:
  - Green: #2ECC40 (Success/Positive)
  - Red: #FF4136 (Warning/Negative)

## Design Elements
- Clean lines and minimalistic design.
- Use of high-quality materials for durability and comfort.
- Ergonomic furniture to support long hours of work.
